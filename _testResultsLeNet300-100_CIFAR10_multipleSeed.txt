v3 compared the end-to-end pipelines under a fixed accuracy budget with a small (0.5 pp) neuron-phase cap. 
As a result fewer neuron steps followed by edge pruning. 
v4:
(i) run the neuron phase to completion before the edge phase (NEURON_SEQUENTIAL=1), 
(ii) enlarge the neuron-phase cap to about 0.8 × the budget (NEURON_TOL=0.008), and 
(iii) add a matched-neuron-count design (NEURON_MAX_FRAC = value), where both heuristics remove the same fraction of hidden neurons, to isolate selection quality from the stopping rule. A [neuron end] snapshot records the model at the end of the neuron phase.

Final state: the edge phase is very efficient and saturates (about −97%). 
IterCC ends at 3.0% remaining against Taylor’s 2.4%, a gap of 0.6 points that is not significant with 3 seeds (t of about 1.1).
